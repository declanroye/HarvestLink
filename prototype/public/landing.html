A CONNECTION BEYOND THE BORDER

Good harvests deserve
more than a good guess.

Small harvests, scattered buyer messages and uncertain transport costs make selling difficult. HarvestLink brings those pieces into one conversation, helping farmers understand their options before committing.

The Bonfim–Lethem tomato example demonstrates Portuguese-speaking farmers and English-speaking buyers across northern Brazil and Guyana. It is a starting point for a wider regional network, with more crops, communities and markets.

WHEN THE SIGNAL DISAPPEARS

The work doesn’t have to.

On your phone

A cached companion runs the small intent model locally. Keep drafting harvests, confirming records and reviewing saved market information. Close and reopen to continue.

When you reconnect

Link your WhatsApp account, review local changes and explicitly submit them. Revision checks surface conflicts and operation receipts make retries safe.

Clear limits

WhatsApp itself needs connectivity. Offline market data can be stale. Buyer acceptance, carrier booking and trade clearance are never inferred from a saved record.
